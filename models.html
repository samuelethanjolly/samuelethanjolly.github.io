To the Chapter Master of the Antonines, Antonius Aurus

We are persued. Departing Atarhyll, we took planetside bombardment. The tactics of the Gene-Warden Astartes allowed us success at reclaiming territory, yet has become our undoing. The reports from my Acolytes are brief and exact: the Gene-Wardens have fallen to heresy. Their numbers are split, but the majority shield their eyes from the Emperor's Light. I bring condolences as well. In this fall, they have betrayed their Chapter Master Ethus Sejan. I believe that you and he were close, having fought off numerous incursion together. We believe the betrayal came from his 1st Company Captain Tatikos.
We will begin an examination of the gene-seed of all allied chapters. Considering the Gene-Warden's previous role of supply gene-seed from long-fallen Astartes, and your chapter's reliance upon this, we know you will be fully coperative in undergoing the same examination. Assisting us in this operation are you other close companions, the Consecration Chapter of Astartes. We look forward to seeing your crusade against the chapter that has betrayed the Imperium, trampled on the sectors success and besmurched your honour.
I look forward to our next meeting.
Master Octavianus of the Ordo Astartes.
